How Kitabi Works

Two simple flows — one for buying, one for selling. No fees, no middlemen.

Buying a book

Find what you need in a few clicks.

Search or browse

Use the search bar to find books by title, author, or ISBN — or browse by category on the home page.

Open a listing

Tap a book to see photos, condition, price, city and the seller's description.

Contact the seller

Log in and reach out with the contact details shown on the listing. Agree on a place and price.

Meet and collect

Meet in a public place on campus, check the book in person, and pay the seller directly.

Selling a book

List in under a minute.

Create your listing

Click Sell a Book, fill in the title, author, category, condition, price and city.

Add photos

Upload up to 10 photos of the book (cover, spine, any damage). Listings with photos sell faster.

Publish

Hit Post Book. Your listing appears on the home page and on your profile.

Hand it over

When a buyer contacts you, arrange a meetup. Mark the listing as Sold once it's gone.

Safety tips

Small habits that keep everyone safe.

  • Meet in public — on campus, in a cafe, or anywhere with people around.
  • Check before you pay — flip through the book, confirm the edition, then pay.
  • Use cash — or an instant transfer you both trust. Avoid anything that feels off.
  • Report bad listings — if something looks wrong, email us at support@kitabi.com.
