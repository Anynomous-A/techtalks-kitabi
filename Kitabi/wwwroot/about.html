About Kitabi

Kitabi is a student-first marketplace for buying, selling and sharing university and second-hand books — helping you save money while giving books a second life.

Our mission

Simple, affordable, sustainable.

Every semester, students spend hundreds of dollars on textbooks they'll use for a few months. Meanwhile, shelves at home fill up with books nobody reads again. Kitabi was built to close that gap.

We connect students on the same campus and across cities so books can move from one reader to the next — quickly, locally, and without the middleman markup.

What makes Kitabi different

Built for students, by students.

University-focused

Categories, conditions and prices tailored to what students actually buy and sell each term.

Fair prices

No fees for listing. Sellers set the price, buyers message directly. Everyone wins.

Built for reuse

Every book sold on Kitabi is one less book printed. Good for your wallet, better for the planet.

The team

A small team that loves books and code.

Kitabi started as a university project and grew into a small, focused product. We design, build and maintain everything ourselves — with feedback from students every step of the way.

If you have an idea, a bug to report, or a feature you'd like to see, get in touch. We read everything.
