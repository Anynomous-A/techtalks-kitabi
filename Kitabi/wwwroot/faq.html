Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the things people ask us most.

Is Kitabi free to use?
Yes. Listing a book is completely free, and buyers pay nothing to browse or contact sellers. We don't take a cut from either side.
How do I create an account?
Click Login in the top right, then choose Register. You'll need a name, email, phone number and university. It takes less than a minute.
Can I edit or delete a listing?
Yes. Go to your Profile → My Listings and use the Edit or Delete buttons on any book. You can also mark it as Sold once it's gone.
How do I contact a seller?
Open any listing and you'll see the seller's phone number and university. You need to be logged in to view contact details.
What payment methods should I use?
Cash is safest — pay in person when you meet the seller. If you use a digital transfer, only do it after you've inspected the book and trust the person.
What if a listing looks suspicious?
Don't engage with it. Email us at support@kitabi.com with the listing link, and we'll review it.
I forgot my password. What now?
On the login page, click Forgot password and follow the instructions. If you don't receive a reset email, contact support.
Can I sell books that aren't for university?
Absolutely. As long as it's a real book in the condition you describe, you can list anything — school books, novels, reference guides, anything readable.
How many photos can I upload?
Up to 10 photos per listing. JPG, PNG or GIF, up to 5 MB each. Photos of the actual book (not stock images) get the best results.
Do you deliver books?
No — Kitabi is a local marketplace. Buyers and sellers arrange their own meetup, usually on campus or in a nearby public place.

Still have a question?

We're happy to help. Reach out on the contact page, or email us directly at support@kitabi.com.
