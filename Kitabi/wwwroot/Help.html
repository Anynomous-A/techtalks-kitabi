Kitabi Help Center

Soft, simple guidance on how to use the site.

1. Getting Started

Register or log in to your account.

Use the search bar to find books by title, author, or ISBN.

2. Navigation Guide

  1. Home: Overview and featured books.
  2. Browse Books: Search and filter listings.
  3. Sell a Book: Post your own listing.
  4. Profile: Manage your account and listings.
  5. Help: Access this guide anytime.

3. FAQ

How do I reset my password?

Click “Login” → “Forgot Password” and follow the instructions.

Can I edit or delete my book listing?

Yes, go to your Profile and select the listing you want to update or remove.
